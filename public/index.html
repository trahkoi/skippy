Randomize replaces a block’s slots with a random mix of steps, hops, and actions. Numbered beats always have a symbol; & and a may be empty. Random two beats adds a new random block. Random six counts adds three random two-beat blocks. On an untouched sheet, it replaces the starting empty block; otherwise it appends to your rhythm. Click any slot to adjust it.
